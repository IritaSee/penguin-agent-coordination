\begin{abstract}
This paper introduces the Uphill Priority Protocol (UPP), a coordination framework for multi-agent systems inspired by the energy-efficient trail etiquette of penguins. In biological systems, penguins ascending a slope are granted the right of way to minimize the energy cost of restarting movement against gravity. We translate this principle into the domain of Agentic AI, where agents with higher computational load or reasoning depth are prioritized to prevent context-switching costs and token wastage.
\end{abstract}
